Account & Data Deletion Request for [Your App Name]

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can request permanent deletion at any time.

How to Request:

Send an email to your-support-email@example.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request.

Please send the email from the Google email address associated with your app account so we can verify ownership.

What Data Will Be Deleted:

  • User profile details (Name, Email, Account ID)
  • App activity and stored personal settings
  • Associated database records

Data Retention & Processing Time:

Your request will be processed within 30 days. Once deleted, all personal data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
