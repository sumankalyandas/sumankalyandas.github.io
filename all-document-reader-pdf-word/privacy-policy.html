Overview & Core Commitment

Welcome to All Document Reader: PDF, Word (hereinafter referred to as the "Application" or "App"), operated by Mobility Document Apps Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us").

Our Core Privacy Promise: We design our application with a Local-First Architecture. We do NOT upload, transfer, scan, or store your documents, files, PDFs, spreadsheets, or slides on remote servers. All parsing, rendering, indexation, and reading take place 100% locally on your personal device.
2

Information Collection & Device Permissions

To provide file reading, organizing, and management functionalities, the App requires certain operating system permissions. We only request permissions strictly essential for the core utility of reading your files.

Storage Access Permissions (Android & iOS)

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

Why it is needed: Enables the app to scan, list, open, rename, bookmark, and render documents (.pdf, .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .txt) stored in your device's internal storage or SD card.

Strict Boundary: Under no circumstances are the names, contents, or metadata of your personal files collected, transmitted, or monitored by us. Access remains entirely on-device.

Device & Telemetry Data

When you use the App, our integrated third-party analytics and ad network SDKs may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information, including:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version (e.g., Android 14, iOS 17)
  • IP address (used to infer broad geographic region such as country/city)
  • Network connection status (Wi-Fi vs Mobile Cellular)
  • App crashes, ANRs (Application Not Responding), and rendering failure logs
  • Advertising ID (Google AAID or Apple IDFA, subject to your consent settings)

Data Storage & Security

We prioritize the protection of your digital environment. Because our App functions primarily offline and processes files locally, the risk of cloud-based intercept or server breaches involving your confidential files is mitigated at the architectural level.

Local Sandboxing

Temporary rendering caches are kept within Android/iOS app sandboxes, isolated from unauthorized external apps.

Encrypted Transit

All diagnostic logs sent to Firebase and ad requests to AdMob utilize modern TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols.

Zero Cloud Duplication

We do not operate cloud storage relays; your documents stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

User Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California), you hold specific statutory privacy rights regarding your data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request disclosure of any telemetry data linked to your advertising identifier.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can clear app storage, cache, or reset your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Information: We do not sell personal data or trade document contents to data brokers.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can withdraw file storage permissions anytime via your OS settings (Settings > Apps > All Document Reader > Permissions).
7

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU member states). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children. If we become aware that personal information of a child has been inadvertently gathered without parental consent verification, we will promptly take steps to delete such records from our diagnostic analytics systems.
